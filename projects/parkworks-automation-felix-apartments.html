Project Overview

This project documents the automated parking system installed by Parkworks Mechanical Systems at the Felix Apartments, a residential development in Santa Rosa. The brief was the same as the earlier Parkworks garages: portray the system artfully and realistically inside the building it serves, so the client can show developers and residents what the installation looks like in use.

The set covers the garage as a whole and the system in its parts: the drive aisle between the rows of platforms, the yellow guard mesh and control columns, cars sitting on the lower platforms with the upper pallets raised above them, the wall-mounted EV chargers that serve the lift positions, and close frames of the lift beam, drive chain and locking hardware. Daylight through the garage glazing carried most of the wider views.

Creative Process

Coverage Plan
Two scales in one visit: the garage as a space, shot down the drive aisle and across the rows of platforms, and the system as engineering, shot close on the columns, guards, chargers and drive hardware. The client needs both, because the first sells the installation to a developer and the second explains it to an engineer.
Light
The garage's glazing let daylight in across the platforms, which carried the wider views without added light and kept the yellow safety guards and the concrete reading true. Detail frames were shot where the system's own fixtures lit the hardware.
Composition
Straight lines kept straight. Columns, beams and guard rails run through every frame, so the camera stayed level and the verticals stayed vertical, the same discipline as architectural interiors. Cars on the platforms give the system its scale.
Post-Production
Color correction for consistency across the set with a realistic rendering of the system, its finishes and the garage. No retouching of the installation itself.
Delivery
High-resolution files for marketing, proposals and presentations, delivered with usage rights for Parkworks Mechanical Systems.
