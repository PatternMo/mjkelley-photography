About This Set

Auction platforms ask for a hundred to two hundred photographs per car, and most of them are not hero shots. This is the full set for a 1957 Chevrolet Corvette, shown in auction listing order. Only the plate-only frames are left out here, though the plate itself shows in the exterior frames; the Chevrolet body tag and its serial number are in.

The point of a set like this is trust. The hero frames get a bidder to click. The detail frames let them see the condition of the car for themselves.

  • Vehicle: 1957 Chevrolet Corvette
  • Location: Marin County, CA
  • Shot: May 2026, one session
  • Frames delivered: 218
  • Brief: full auction-platform shot list, exterior to undercarriage
  • Not in the public set: plate-only frames (the plate still shows in the exterior frames)

Planning to auction your car? I have shot for all the major auction sites and will deliver every image on their shot list, developed to the standards in their photo briefs.
